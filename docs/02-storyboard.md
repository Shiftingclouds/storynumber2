# NUIT BLANCHE: Storyboard (revision 2, the long version)

Nine nights, from Friday 20 February to Saturday 28 February 2026 (Nuit blanche). Each night is a chapter with a
pixel-art title card. Nights Five and Six exist in two versions, depending on the midpoint split.

**Length target:** about twice *Heldwater* per playthrough, around 100,000 words read and 250+ pages, out of about
180,000 words written.

## The rule for every scene

Every scene has to earn its place by doing at least one of these:

1. **Turns a relationship**, visibly, so a later scene reads it.
2. **Plants or pays off** a clue, an object, a memory or a promise.
3. **Asks a choice** whose flag something later reads (see the ledger at the end).

Quiet scenes are not filler. They're where the relationships turn and the objects get handed over, and they're
where the player decides who they are. Every quiet scene below lists its payoff.

Each night has the same shape:

- an **opening** (quiet: aftermath, texts, somebody at a kitchen table);
- a **set piece**;
- a **relationship scene** (quiet);
- a **complication**;
- a **hook**;
- **dawn** (texts, a last image).

## Night One: The Door (Friday) *(written: about 13,000 words)*

1. **Cold open: the dentist.** Establishes voice, craft and flirting. (`dentist`) Callbacks: his text at the Thaw,
   the Nuit blanche crowd, the epilogue.
2. **LIGNE 3. The phrase.** (`c_phrase`) Callback: the Keyman (7): "I called you from the Line's phone."
3. **Identity.** The mirror (the look creator), name, your twenties (+15 to a skill).
4. **The notebook** (quiet): *"Six notes. The song is the key. The key is a Lacroix. Pour le fort, voir S."* The next
   page is torn out. (`has_notebook`) Payoffs: another way through the lock; "always begin on the fourth" at the
   change-ringing puzzle (6A); the Keyman has the torn page (7).
5. **The bridge memory** (quiet): the night he rode along at twelve and never remembered the drive home. (`n1_memory`)
   Payoff: the Thaw (4) fills the gap.
6. **The fort. The phone** (quiet): 911 (static and humming), Marc-André, the dentist, or nobody. (`called`)
7. **The envelope.** Twenty paper hundreds from before 2011, and **a Polaroid**: a man and a twelve-year-old boy in
   front of this door, *août 2009*. You don't remember it. (`has_photo`, `photo`) Payoffs: the Thaw (4), the Keyman (7).
8. **The lock.** Finesse, the notebook, force, or (New Game+) memory.
9. **Nadim in chains.** His name, the bands, two questions. (`n1_nadim`, `n1_gave_name`)
10. **3:33: the bells.** Lazare and Agathe. The bell fails. Truth, lie, joke or flirt. (`n1_lied`) The Bourdon's call.
11. **Wolves on the ice.** Luc, the new wolf, goes for Agathe. You step between them, pull her down, shout, or freeze.
    (`saved_agathe`) Payoffs: Agathe spares Luc and Manon at the siege (5B); Agathe's side (6A).
12. **The standoff.** Go with Lazare, go with Dario, run, or play them off each other. (`n1_with`)
13. **The rides** (quiet), each with two choices. (`n1_ride`)
14. **Chez Normande.** Normande, a lutin's braid (`lutin`), Fleurette, questions.
15. **Dawn.** Texts you can answer. Nadim at the window (`window_talk`; you can invite him in).

## Night Two: The Missing Line (Saturday) *(target about 16,000)*

1. **Evening** (quiet): the photo on the kitchen table. Texts (Marc-André if you called; the dentist). The care home
   calls: Lucille is asking for Serge.
2. **Mémé** (quiet, pivotal). She takes you for Serge: *"You went back to the fort. You promised Papa."* Play along as
   Serge, or tell her who you are. As Serge, you learn more (`serge_promise`). As yourself, she knows you for one
   lucid minute. Either way, *"A Lacroix will open it, and a Lacroix will have to close it"* (`c_close`), and she
   gives you the **little brass key** she has worn on a chain for fifty years: *"He said, give it to whoever opens
   the door."* (`meme_key`) Payoffs: at the lock (9) it opens the bands, so the Hush can be *remade* instead of only
   closed; her own last-night scene (8); the epilogue.
3. **Down to the Line.** Fleurette, the 24-hour bagel bakery on Saint-Viateur, the oven room, the stairs.
4. **The market** (set piece): the stalls, favors (codex), and you're famous. How you handle the crowd.
5. **Aimé** (quiet): Secondaire 3; what ghouls are; kindness earns `favor_aime`.
6. **The Conductor and Nadim's name.** Nadim is at the Conductor's stall, trying to buy back his **brass name-plate**,
   locked in a strongbox that only a Lacroix can open (Aurèle made it). The Conductor offers you the job.
   - Open it and **hand it over** as agreed: `favor_clarke`; Nadim remembers.
   - **Steal it** for Nadim (Hands or Wits): the Conductor remembers.
   - **Make the price of your work be that he gives it back** (Charm): the Conductor's redemption begins
     (`clarke_moved`).
   - Or spend Aimé's favor to buy it outright.

   (`plate_got`, `plate_how`) Nadim pays the **first wish**.
7. **Ruari** delivers Honora's invitation. Kandi bracelets (`c_kandi_worn`).
8. **The Keyman** (quiet): he cuts you a key you didn't ask for and hums six notes. (`c_six_notes`) New Game+: *"Papa?"*
9. **The body** in a service tunnel: Mireille Caron. The bell bruise, the wolf hair; take a sample; Aimé tastes
   her. The crowd blames the wolves; Dario arrives; the Carillon arrive. The Conductor rules. Defend the pack, stay
   out, or back the Carillon. (`n2_stance`)
10. **The escort** (quiet): a long walk in the snow with Lazare, with Dario, or with both (the scarf).
11. **Dawn.** Nadim's message in the frost; Ruari's text; the photo goes on the fridge, or in a drawer.

## Night Four: Mardi Gras (Monday) *(target about 16,000)*

1. **Getting dressed** (quiet): Fleurette dresses you and lends you the cufflinks of *"the only man who ever bought
   me a real diamond."* (`cufflinks`) Payoffs: Rose notices; Last Call. The **deduction board** opens.
2. **The party** (set piece): everyone is there. You overhear Honora and the Conductor: *"The key will cooperate, or
   the old man will do."* (`overheard`)
3. **The dance**: five rounds, the meter (`danced_won` or `owe_rose`, `c_accord_signers`).
4. **The coat room**: Lazare and Dario. *"Don't call me that."* (`know_affair`, `three_kiss`)
5. **Midnight: THE THAW.** You're twelve again at the fort, with your father singing six notes; the photo makes sense
   (`mem_notes`). Lazare remembers he is Enzo. The street sees. The dentist texts, and Marc-André calls. The
   journal melts.
6. **1 a.m.:** the bells close it again, and the Bourdon declares war. **The split** (`path`).

## Night Seven: The Compagnie (Thursday) *(target about 16,000)*

1. **The Keyman** (quiet, pivotal). The photo, the notebook's torn page (he has it), the call from the Line's phone.
   Father and son. (`keyman_known`, `keyman_forgiven`) Ruari comes for him (`keyman_safe` / `keyman_taken`).
2. **The dinner and the accusation.**
3. **Rose's offer.**
4. **Mémé, at 3 a.m.** (quiet): she knows you by your real name.

## Night Eight: The Eve (Friday) *(target about 16,000)*

1. **The council**: `intent`, and Gisèle's four requirements.
2. **The plan**: the Door, the Crowd, the Bells, Beside you.
3. **The last night** (steam): Lazare, Dario, both, Rose, the friends, Mémé, or alone.
4. **Nadim's smoke dream**: a wish if you have room for one.

## Night Nine: Nuit Blanche (Saturday) *(target about 18,000)*

The crowd, the bells, the door, the vault, the choice at the lock:

- **rebound**: close it on Nadim;
- **lock**: take his place;
- **gardien**: your father takes it (only if the Keyman is safe and forgiven);
- **fallen**: break it forever;
- **remade**: needs Gisèle, a voice, Nadim's consent, and Mémé's key;
- **rose**: the devil's bargain;
- **the great wish**.

## Endings (23)

These are the 22 in `config.js` plus **Le Gardien** (*"Your father took the lock. You visit every Thursday, the way
he visited Nadim."*). Then **Morning**: a portrait card for every person you met, and what became of them.
